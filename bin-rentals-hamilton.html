What can go in each bin

  • Clean fill bins: clean dirt, topsoil, grass or sod only. No garbage whatsoever.

  • Clean rubble or asphalt bins: concrete and asphalt can't be mixed unless arranged with dispatch when you order, and can't contain any garbage or dirt.

  • Garbage bins: household or construction waste.

  • Never allowed: hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, liquids, tires, propane tanks, medical or radioactive material.

Extra charges may apply if these guidelines aren't followed. Read the full service agreement.

Hamilton Bin Rental FAQ

Do you rent bins in Hamilton?

Yes. We drop off and pick up bins across Hamilton, including Stoney Creek, Ancaster, Dundas, Waterdown / Flamborough, Binbrook and Hamilton Mountain.

What bin sizes do you have?

Clean fill and clean rubble or asphalt bins come in 6, 16 and 20 yard sizes. Garbage bins come in 6, 14, 20 and 40 yard sizes. Dimensions and included tonnage are listed under Bin Types and Sizes.

What can I put in a bin?

Clean fill bins take clean dirt, topsoil, grass or sod only. Concrete and asphalt go in separate bins unless you arrange otherwise with dispatch, with no garbage or dirt. Garbage bins take household or construction waste. Hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, liquids, tires and propane tanks are not allowed in any bin.
